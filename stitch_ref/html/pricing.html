Academic Licensing FAQ

Do you offer institutional student discounts?

expand_more
Yes! We partner with over 200 universities globally. If you register with a verified .edu or .ac.uk email, you automatically receive 40% off any Pro annual plan.

Can I use Grant funds to pay for LaTeX Genius?

expand_more
Absolutely. We provide itemized VAT/Tax invoices compliant with NIH, NSF, and ERC grant reporting requirements. We also support Purchase Orders for department-wide licensing.

How does the "Smart Bibliography" handle citations?

expand_more
Our AI indexes millions of papers. Simply paste a DOI or a URL, and we'll fetch the metadata and format it instantly in APA, MLA, IEEE, or any custom .bst style your journal requires.

Is my unpublished research data secure?

expand_more
Security is our priority. All documents are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit. We never use your private papers to train our AI models without explicit opt-in.
